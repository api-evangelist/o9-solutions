# o9 Solutions

> Enterprise AI platform for integrated planning and decision-making. o9's Digital Brain unifies supply chain, commercial, and financial planning on the Enterprise Knowledge Graph (EKG) — an always-on digital twin of the enterprise — enabling real-time sensing, scenario simulation, and connected decisions across strategic, tactical, and operational horizons.

o9 Solutions was founded in 2009 by Sanjiv Sidhu (Chairman, formerly founder of i2 Technologies) and Chakri Gottemukkala (CEO). The company is headquartered in Dallas, Texas, with offices globally, and serves 30+ industry verticals. Named customers include Keurig Dr Pepper, T-Mobile, Comcast, Philips, Pepsico, Pirelli, Coca-Cola Bottlers Japan, and Marks & Spencer.

The Digital Brain platform combines the patented Enterprise Knowledge Graph, AI/ML forecasting, generative and agentic AI (composite agents built on atomic agents), and modular "building block" solutions across supply chain, integrated business planning, revenue growth management, and P&L planning. o9 is a Gartner Magic Quadrant-recognized vendor across Supply Chain Planning, Retail Merchandise Financial Planning, and Decision Intelligence Platforms.
